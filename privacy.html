Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Data we access

When the owner connects their account, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (user.info.basic) and uses the video.upload permission only to send the owner's approved videos to their own account.

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared, sold or sent to third parties. The App does not collect any data about viewers or other users.

Deletion

Revoking access in the TikTok or Google account settings, or deleting the local token file, removes all access. No other copy exists.
